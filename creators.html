Three video ideas to start from

These are starting points, not lines to read out. Change anything so it sounds like you, and only keep what's true for you. For the parts that show the app, ask me (Noah) for a screen recording to use.

The C-drama subtitle trap"Is it just me, or are subtitles actually the biggest trap?"

  1. Show

    Close-up, talking to camera. You're holding a new phone or tablet, with a corner of the clear screen protector pinched between your fingers.

    On-screen text

    just me / subtitles / biggest trap?

    Say

    "Is it just me, or are subtitles actually the biggest trap?"

  2. Show

    Slowly peel the protector back, close to the mic. Cut to you looking frustrated, with a drama playing on the TV behind you.

    On-screen text

    watching shows / barely any vocab growth / real TV way too fast / textbooks boring / like me,

    Say

    "You spend hours watching C-dramas, but you're just reading English. Barely any vocab growth. Real Chinese is way too fast and textbooks are just boring. If you're stuck like me,"

  3. Show

    Screen recording of Ting opening, with a clip playing.

    On-screen text

    how I survive / tingpanda.com / real Chinese clips

    Say

    "this is how I survive. Ting lets you watch real Chinese videos with interactive words."

  4. Show

    Screen recording. Tap a word in the subtitles: the pinyin and English pop up, and it's saved to your flashcards. Add a ding in the edit.

    On-screen text

    tap any word / pinyin / auto-flashcards / remember it / HSK levels

    Say

    "Just tap a word for the pinyin and meaning. It automatically saves it to your flashcards, so you actually remember it. You can even filter by HSK level, so it's not overwhelming."

  5. Show

    Back to camera, looking relieved, with Ting open on your phone.

    On-screen text

    A few words from your own line.

    Say

    One true thing Ting has done for you, like a word you now catch when you hear it.

  6. Show

    Point to where the Save button sits on TikTok.

    On-screen text

    Save this / study sesh / follow for more

    Say

    "Save this for your next study sesh and follow for more tips."

Filming notes: use natural light facing you, with a good mic close to the phone for the peel. Keep the cuts fast during the demo, and highlight key words in the captions in red or yellow. Start on a low-fi, slightly frustrated beat, then switch to a brighter lo-fi beat when the app appears.

Good to know

How many followers do I need?

There's no minimum. A small audience that really wants to learn Chinese can be a great fit.

How do you know who came from me?

Each subscriber who redeems your code is counted towards you. Someone who subscribes without entering it can't be traced back to you, so make the code easy to find.

What counts as revenue?

What Ting actually receives from Apple for those subscribers, which is after Apple's commission, taxes and refunds.

Does it cost me anything, or tie me down?

No cost and no exclusivity. You can work with other apps too, and stop whenever you like.

Where is Ting available?

On iPhone and iPad, in most countries. It isn't available in mainland China, so it suits audiences outside China best.
